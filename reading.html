What I'm reading

  In 2025, I realized that since I’m a researcher and will have to travel a lot and live wherever work takes me, I wouldn’t be able to build up a book collection. So I decided to at least take notes on the books I’ve read since 2025 and post them on my website. I add a book to the list if I found it interesting in some way, so it doesn’t necessarily mean I liked it.

  • "Performance Works of Lia Haraki", Lia Haraki, Erica Charalambous
  • "Paris: Ronis", Willy Ronis
  • "Bullshit Jobs", David Graeber
  • "Time for Immediate Photography", Maria Bavykina, Vladislav Efimov
  • "Not Working: Why We Have to Stop", Josh Cohen
  • "Jaws in Space: Powerful Pitching for Film and TV Screenwriters", Charles Harris
  • "Blue: The History of a Color", Michel Pastoureau
  • "So Good They Can’t Ignore You: Why Skills Trump Passion in the Quest for Work You Love", Cal Newport
  • "Anything but Work: Stories About Contemporary Art", Andrea Bellini
  • "A Brief History of Thought: A Philosophical Guide to Living", Luc Ferry
  • "Lewis W. Hine. America at Work", Lewis W. Hine, Peter Walther
  • "Design Today", Maria Savostyanova
  • "Design and Form: The Basic Course at the Bauhaus and Later", Johannes Itten
  • "Париж глазами художников", Жерар Денизо
  • "Архитектура. Искусство понимать", Марина Сергеевна Яровая
  • "Искусство XX века. Ключи к пониманию: события, художники, эксперименты", Алина Аксёнова
